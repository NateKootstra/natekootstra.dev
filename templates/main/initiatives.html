Our Initiatives

The Sabre Bytes robotics team was established in 2001 at Sandwich Secondary School (LaSalle, Ontario). We compete each year in the FIRST Robotics Competition with the goal of making the best robot possible. Our members (from Sandwich and from other schools) work together to design, build, wire, and program these robots.
However, building robots isn't the only thing we do. Each year, team 772 competes to try and win the prestigious FIRST Impact Award. This requires us to document and present the many initiatives we take on to spread STEAM and help our community.
Funded by our generous sponsors (who can be found on the Sponsor Information page), our team helps students develop their skills and build friendships alongside robots.
